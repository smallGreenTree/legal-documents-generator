Making generated indictment text less repetitive
Simple version
19 August 2026


WHAT THIS SYSTEM DOES
---------------------

It does three steps, for every kind of case (procurement, bribery,
tax, insider trading, and so on):

  1. Invent the people and companies (names, addresses, VAT numbers,
     amounts).

  2. Ask the model to write a legal document using only those
     details. It is not allowed to make up extra people, invoices,
     or events.

  3. Mark every place those names appear. That marking is the gold
     for NER.

That rule in step 2 is on purpose. If the model invents an invoice
number, gold will not know about it, and NER evaluation breaks.


WHY THE TEXT SOUNDS BAD
-----------------------

Step 1 gives a list of names. It does not give a story.

A real indictment needs a story: who did what, through which
company, over which dates, what the charges are, and what kinds of
evidence exist.

The model is then told: write a long document, and do not invent.
So it only has names and a short paragraph. It copies that
paragraph into every chapter until the page is full.

That is why persons, companies, history, charges, facts, evidence
and assessment all sound the same. It is not that gold is wrong.
It is that the writer was given almost nothing to write about, and
was forced to write a lot.

Filling in one case file by hand does not fix this. The next
scenario has the same empty story.


THE THREE CHANGES
-----------------

Think of it as: names -> story -> writing.

Today you have names, then you jump straight to writing.


1. Invent the story automatically, from the names, for every case

   After people and companies are created, and before any chapter
   is written, the software should also write down:

   - the time period of the offence
   - who runs which company
   - who told whom to do what
   - money moving from company A to company B (when there is a
     second company)
   - the charges
   - the kinds of evidence (invoices, bank records, and so on),
     not fake exhibit numbers

   This is not a human editing one YAML. It is a shared step that
   every scenario goes through. Procurement, bribery, tax: same
   step, different names.

   Until this exists, every case is "a list of names, please write
   20 pages".


2. Give each chapter only what it needs, and let it be short

   A persons chapter should see names, dates of birth, addresses.
   It should not see the whole fraud story. If it does, it will
   retell the fraud.

   A facts chapter should see the story (who did what, money,
   dates). An evidence chapter should see the kinds of evidence.

   Also: stop requiring a long word count. If the persons chapter
   only has two people, a short identification paragraph is
   correct. Forcing 450 words makes the model repeat itself.

   So: each chapter gets a smaller pack of facts, and it may stop
   when those facts are used.


3. If a chapter copies itself, make it write again

   The system can already notice "this sentence appeared twice".
   Today it mostly writes that down in a report and keeps the
   repeated text.

   Turn that into: the chapter is not finished until the copies
   are gone.

   This does not invent a better plot. It only stops padding.
   It only works well after 1 and 2, because then the model has
   something else to say instead of repeating.


WHAT "REALISTIC" MEANS HERE
---------------------------

It does not mean a full real CPS file with invented emails and
warrant numbers. Those would be invisible to gold.

It means:

  - each chapter does a different job
  - the story is used in the facts, not pasted into the persons
    list
  - the document is as long as the story, not as long as a word
    target
  - names appear in normal sentences, not in the same sentence
    copied ten times

A small case should produce a short document. A bigger case
(more people, more companies, more money movements) should
produce a longer one. Both should read like legal prose, not
like the same paragraph seven times.


IF THIS IS STILL UNCLEAR
------------------------

The whole note is one sentence:

  The model is asked to write a long court document from a list
  of names. Give it a story first, give each chapter only its
  part of that story, and do not force it to keep writing after
  the story is used up.
